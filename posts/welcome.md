I'm starting this blog as a place to document what I'm learning and working on during my PhD in semiconductor materials and optoelectronic devices. I'll be writing about topics that come up in my research, from semiconductor growth and device physics to characterization, modeling, and the challenges involved in developing next-generation devices.

Expect a mix of technical notes, reflections on papers I'm reading, things I'm learning along the way, and occasional updates on my research. This isn't meant to be a collection of polished papers — it's more of a working notebook where I can document ideas, questions, and things that I find interesting.

If something here is useful, or if you'd like to discuss any of it further, feel free to reach out through the Contact page.

Thanks!
