Surface Charging–Induced Profile Evolution in 3D NAND Etching: Linking Reactor Conditions to Feature Evolution
Siddharth Mohanty, Vatsal Thakor, Om Maheshwari, Nihar R. Mohapatra
SISPAD 2026 · Semiconductor process simulation · Surface charging · 3D NAND

This paper presents a simulation-based investigation into how surface charging during plasma etching drives profile distortion in 3D NAND memory structures. The work builds a link from reactor-level plasma parameters down to the microscale feature evolution observed in fabricated devices.
